# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: How do editors get paid on ClipRewards?
A: Editors apply to campaigns created by YouTube creators. Once accepted by the creator, the editor cuts engaging Shorts from the creator's video, uploads them to YouTube, and submits the live video link. Our automated background workers poll YouTube's API every 3 minutes and credit money directly to the editor's wallet per 1,000 verified views.

### Q: Can an editor submit multiple clips to the same campaign?
A: Yes! As long as the creator has accepted your application and the campaign budget and your editor cap have not been reached, you can submit multiple distinct clips. Each clip must have a unique YouTube URL.

### Q: Can I submit the same clip to multiple different campaigns?
A: No. The system enforces a strict unique video constraint across the entire platform. Each YouTube video ID can only be linked to a single campaign to protect creator investments.

### Q: What is the typical reward rate per 1,000 views?
A: Reward rates typically range from **$1.50 to $5.00+ per 1,000 views**, depending on the campaign's niche (gaming, technology, education, entertainment) and creator budget.

### Q: Do YouTube Shorts qualify for payouts?
A: Absolutely! In fact, vertical YouTube Shorts (9:16 aspect ratio) are the primary vehicle for campaigns because of their high viral distribution.

### Q: How do creators protect their budget?
A: Creators specify a total budget (e.g. $1,000) and a maximum payout per editor (e.g. $200). Once the budget is exhausted, rewards automatically halt. Creators can also pause campaigns at any moment.

### Q: How do I link my YouTube channel?
A: Go to **Editor Studio ➔ YouTube Channels** and click **Connect YouTube Account** to authenticate via Google OAuth, or manually submit your public YouTube Channel ID (e.g. `UC...`).
