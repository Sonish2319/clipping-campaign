# YouTube Shorts & Video Clipping Guidelines

## 1. Technical Formatting Specifications
1. **Aspect Ratio**: 
   - Recommended: Vertical 9:16 (1080x1920) for YouTube Shorts.
   - Standard 16:9 is acceptable only if explicitly requested in the campaign instructions.
2. **Clip Duration**:
   - Platform default: 15 seconds to 60 seconds for Shorts.
   - Always adhere to the specific campaign's `min_clip_duration_sec` and `max_clip_duration_sec`. Clips outside this range are rejected during submission.
3. **Resolution & Audio**:
   - Minimum 1080p resolution.
   - Crisp, balanced audio mix (voiceover must be legible over background music).

## 2. High-Retention Editing Standards
To maximize viral potential and viewer retention on YouTube Shorts:
1. **Strong 3-Second Hook**: Open with high action, a compelling question, or emotional peak to reduce swipe-away rates.
2. **Kinetic Typography / Subtitles**: Add synchronized, animated captions. More than 70% of Shorts viewers watch with sound off or low volume.
3. **Pacing & Cuts**: Remove dead pauses, silence, and filler words. Use punch-ins, zoom transitions, sound effects (SFX), and B-roll where appropriate.
4. **Visual Polish**: Color grade, correct aspect ratio framing (no awkward black pillar-boxing around facecam), and balance dialogue levels.

## 3. Disallowed Content & Practices
1. **Raw Unedited Dumps**: Taking a 5-minute slice of a stream and uploading with zero cuts, subtitles, or editing is strictly disallowed.
2. **Misleading Metadata / Clickbait**: Do not use deceptive titles or thumbnails that misrepresent the content of the creator's video.
3. **Hate Speech & Explicit Content**: Any hate speech, harassment, vulgarity, copyright strikes, or violations of YouTube's Terms of Service will result in immediate termination of the editor's account.
4. **Copyright Infringement**: Use copyright-free or YouTube Audio Library background music to avoid Content ID claims on the creator's assets.
